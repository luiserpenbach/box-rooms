Survive

  • Kills build your multiplier — don't let it cool down. Score unlocks new weapons and stat upgrades.
  • Zombies see in a cone and hear gunfire. Sneak past sleepers, or pick your fights.
  • A groaning zombie alerts the pack. Screamers alert everything.
  • Shove knocks zombies back and interrupts their attacks.
  • Barricades (wood) slow the horde. Build your own with Walls, blow them open with explosives.
  • In co-op, a downed partner can be revived by standing next to them.
  • Shoot fireballs out of the air. Lure zombies into Bloater blasts.
